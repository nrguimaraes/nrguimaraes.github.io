Nuno Ricardo Guimarães

Researcher at INESC TEC · LIAAD · Porto, Portugal

nuno.r.guimaraes@inesctec.pt

I am a researcher specialising in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Information Retrieval, and Generative AI. My work focuses on extracting, analysing, and interpreting information from text, with applications in healthcare, news media, disinformation, and public administration.

My research ranges from misinformation and social media analysis to multilingual narrative extraction, language models, and clinical decision support. I am currently a researcher at the Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence and Decision Support (LIAAD), INESC TEC, where I also lead and co-lead multidisciplinary research projects.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Porto and teach courses on AI, Data Science, Programming, and related topics at higher education institutions in Porto.

Research

I develop computational methods that make unstructured language data more useful, interpretable, and accessible. My research combines methodological advances in NLP and machine learning with applications involving societal challenges.

01

Language Models & Generative AI

Large language models, multilingual NLP, evaluation of model capabilities, information extraction, synthetic data, and responsible AI.

02

Disinformation & Narrative Analysis

Reliability of social media content and accounts, persuasion techniques, claim detection, and extraction and explanation of news narratives.

03

AI for Healthcare

Clinical language processing, medical information retrieval, patient journeys, clinical narratives, and decision-support applications.

04

AI for Public Administration

European Portuguese language resources, municipal meeting analysis, information retrieval, transparency, and citizen engagement.
